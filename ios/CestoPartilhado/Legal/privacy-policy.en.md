## 2. What data we collect

- **Account data**: name, email and profile picture, provided by Google when
  you sign in with "Continue with Google".
- **Content you create**: names of shopping lists, stores and items you add,
  and whether each item has been purchased.
- **Sharing data**: email addresses of people you invite to a list.
- **Minimal technical data** automatically generated by the Firebase/Google
  Cloud infrastructure for the service to work (e.g. account identifier).

We do not collect location data, payment data, or special categories of data
(health, origin, etc.).

## 3. What we use the data for

- Creating, displaying and syncing your shopping lists across your devices
  and with the people you share them with.
- Letting you invite other people to a list, and letting them view, add and
  remove items.
- Fulfilling your data export request when you use that feature.
- Keeping the App secure and working correctly.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4. Where and with whom data is stored

Data is stored on Firebase/Google Cloud Platform (Firestore), in a region
located in the European Union ([europe-west1 — Belgium], to confirm). Google
acts as a data processor under its Firebase Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
We do not share your data with any other third party beyond what is
necessary for the service to work (Google/Firebase).

People you share a list with can see that list's content (stores and items)
and your name/email associated with that list, but cannot access your other
lists.

## 5. How long we keep data

- **Active lists**: kept for as long as they exist and are not deleted by
  their creator.
- **Closed lists**: automatically deleted 30 days after being closed.
- **Account**: your profile data is kept for as long as you have an account
  with the App. You may request full deletion at any time (see section 7).
